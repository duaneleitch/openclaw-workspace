# Pattern Signals

Use this file when a draft needs deeper diagnosis before rewriting.
Treat these as signals, not automatic violations.

## Content signals

- significance inflation: pivotal, transformative, game-changing, groundbreaking
- empty prestige references: brand or institution name-dropping without a concrete point
- vague attribution: experts say, studies show, research suggests, industry leaders believe
- decorative analysis verbs: highlighting, showcasing, underscoring, reflecting when they replace substance
- obstacle template: despite challenges, it continues to thrive

## Language signals

- padded verbs: serves as, enables, facilitates, leverages when simpler verbs work
- abstraction stacks: strategy, framework, capability, solution, value, impact packed together
- false precision tone: strong confidence with weak evidence
- synonym cycling: renaming the same thing every sentence
- rule-of-three decoration: stacked triples added for polish rather than clarity

## Rhythm signals

- too many medium-length sentences in a row
- every paragraph opening the same way
- repetitive transition words
- list cadence that feels generated rather than purposeful
- uniform sentence endings or mirrored clause structure

## Communication signals

- chatbot politeness wrappers
- meta commentary about helping, assisting, or exploring
- excessive agreement with the user
- generic conclusions like the future looks bright or time will tell
- hollow calls to action

## Safe fixes

Usually safe:
- remove filler
- swap inflated words for direct ones
- vary sentence length slightly
- delete empty transitions
- convert padded verb phrases into direct verbs

Use caution:
- changing qualifiers
- rewriting claims with numbers or legal meaning
- changing technical nouns
- converting formal writing into conversational writing
- adding warmth that was not present in the source
