# Quick call

Use this for simple, low-risk, highly bounded tasks.

```text
Task: <one simple job>
Source: <material>
Rules:
- Do not guess.
- Return only supported findings.
- If uncertain, say needs_review.
- Keep the answer short and structured.
```

## Use when

- the task is narrow
- the source is clean
- the risk is low
- the desired output is simple extraction, classification, or a short summary

## Do not use when

- the task is customer-facing
- the source is messy
- the task is strategic or ambiguous
- evidence needs to be cited in detail
- the worker could easily drift without tighter framing
