## Reminder rules

- Send reminders at 9:00 a.m. EST.
- Use the cadence: 7 days before, 3 days before, 1 day before, and day of event.
- Interpret all dates in EST.
- For yearly events, schedule recurring reminders each year.
- For one-time events, schedule reminders only for the 2026 date.
- For open-ended recurring tasks, schedule weekly reminders until Duane marks them complete.
