## Why Agents Need Training

**1. Domain Knowledge**
- Agents start with general capabilities but lack company-specific context
- Training embeds product details, processes, terminology, and organizational knowledge
- Example: Your agents need to understand Diversys products, Encorp workflows, your action registers, etc.

**2. Consistency**
- Ensures agents respond in a way that matches your standards
- Maintains tone, format, and quality expectations
- Reduces variability that happens with generic prompting

**3. Task-Specific Reasoning**
- Different tasks need different approaches
- An agent trained on support tickets handles them differently than one trained on sales research
- Training builds pattern recognition for your specific workflows

**4. Reduced Hallucination**
- Grounded knowledge reduces made-up facts
- Referencing actual documents means answers tied to real sources

## How It Works Here

In your setup, "training" typically means:

- **Document ingestion** — Loading Obsidian notes, transcripts, SOPs into the agent's context
- **Skill files** — Structured instructions in `SKILL.md` files that teach specific workflows
- **Memory files** — Curated context like `MEMORY.md`, daily notes, and client folders
- **Reference documents** — Product specs, training materials, meeting transcripts

## Practical Example

An untrained agent asked "What did Shraddha ask about at the Encorp standup?" might:
- Guess or say "I don't have that information"
- Search generically and miss context

**Bottom line:** Training bridges the gap between a general-purpose model and an agent that operates effectively in your specific business environment.
